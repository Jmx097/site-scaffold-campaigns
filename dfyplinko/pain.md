# Pain — dfyplinko.com

## Intent

Name the dual MSP problem: outbound isn't working AND internal ops is eating time. Set up why the combined offer matters (not two separate solutions from two vendors). Buyer leaves thinking "they understand the dual problem. One vendor, one stack, both solved."

---

## Draft (new — V2 pivot)

> **The two problems most MSPs carry at the same time**
>
> **One.** Outbound isn't working. You've tried a BDR. It didn't stick. You've tried an outbound agency. The pipeline report looked good until it didn't. You're spending $8-15k a month on sales tools and a sales person — no clear meetings to show for it.
>
> **Two.** Internal ops is eating time that should go to delivery and sales. Ticket triage takes hours a week that could be automated. Client onboarding is a checklist that lives in someone's head. Proposal generation is copy-paste from last quarter's template. You know Claude or ChatGPT could handle most of this — you don't have 40 hours to become the specialist who sets it up.
>
> **The tax you pay for keeping those separate.** You end up with two contracts, two rhythms, two dashboards, and two sets of tools. The outbound vendor doesn't know your ops. The AI consultant doesn't know your outbound. Nothing compounds.

---

## Critique

- **Three clear blocks: outbound problem, ops problem, "keeping them separate" tax.** Matches sitemap intent.
- **"$8-15k a month on sales tools and a sales person"** — specific, recognizable to the ICP. Keep.
- **"Pipeline report looked good until it didn't"** — peer-tone, landed. MSP founders have lived this. Keep.
- **"Ticket triage," "client onboarding," "proposal generation"** — three specific MSP ops tasks. Matches brief.md "ops that eats bandwidth." Keep.
- **"You know Claude or ChatGPT could handle most of this"** — acknowledges the buyer's awareness. Good. Uses "Claude" which is fine (Anthropic product, brand-aligned).
- **"You don't have 40 hours to become the specialist who sets it up"** — matches positioning.md framing. Keep.
- **"Nothing compounds"** — strong closing three words. Makes the integration argument without belaboring. Keep.
- **Voice check:** Formality 3, assertiveness 5. Structural, declarative, short paragraphs. Good.
- **No AI-hype** ("revolutionize," "transform") — clean.
- **No named customer** — good.
- **Named entity presence:** Claude (×1 as a buyer-side reference, fine), no OpenClaw or Cowork here (they belong in solution.md for entity density). Appropriate.
- **Consider:** Section is a little long — three long paragraphs. Could tighten. But the "two problems + the tax" structure is the argument, hard to cut. Flag for rewrite compression.

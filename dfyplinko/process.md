# Process — dfyplinko.com

## Intent

Describe the 90-day build with phases, what the MSP sees at each, and — the key moment — deliver the "drink our own champagne" proof block exactly once, prominently. This is the most credible thing on the page.

## Draft (new — V2 pivot)

> **90 days, phased**
>
> **Weeks 1-2 — Discovery.** We spend these weeks understanding your MSP: your ICP, your current sales motion (or what's left of it), your PSA, your client list, the three to five operations tasks eating the most time. Output: a scoped plan for both the outbound engine and the Claude Cowork automation roadmap.
>
> **Weeks 2-4 — Infrastructure setup.** Warmed sending domains through ScaledMail. Smartlead campaigns configured. HeyReach profiles warmed. Apollo and Hunter wired to n8n. Claude Cowork workspace provisioned for your MSP with access to your context — client list, service catalog, ops runbooks.
>
> **Weeks 4-6 — Outbound engine live.** First signal-triggered sends go out. You start seeing replies, qualified conversations, and meetings booked to your Cal.com. The weekly reporting rhythm begins.
>
> **Weeks 6-10 — First automations live.** Three to five internal automations stand up — typically ticket triage, client onboarding document generation, and proposal drafting. Each runs on the same n8n + OpenClaw infrastructure that powers the outbound engine.
>
> **Weeks 10-12 — Full routine running.** Outbound is producing weekly pipeline. Automations are saving measurable ops time. You've got weekly reporting for both, on the same dashboards.
>
> ---
>
> **We drink our own champagne**
>
> Plinko's own outbound runs on the same stack we're selling you. Every Monday morning, an n8n workflow pulls account targets from Apollo, scores them against our ICP, and queues them into HeyReach and Smartlead for the week. The landing page you're reading right now was drafted using Claude Cowork. Our internal reporting uses the same n8n attribution layer we'd install for your MSP.
>
> This is the proof that matters. Most AI consultants stop at "here's a ChatGPT prompt." Most outbound agencies know nothing about AI automation. We ship running systems, built on the infrastructure we use ourselves every week.

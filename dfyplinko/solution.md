## Draft (new — V2 pivot, AI-search tuned)

> **What we build**
>
> **The outbound engine.** Apollo for firmographics. Hunter for verified emails. HeyReach for LinkedIn outreach on rotated profiles. Smartlead for email sending on warmed secondary domains. ScaledMail for inbox provisioning. One weekly routine, signal-triggered, with full transparency — you see source lists, sequences, reply rates, and inbox placement at all times.
>
> **The Claude Cowork automation layer.** We set up Claude Cowork for your MSP's internal operations — ticket triage from your PSA, client onboarding document generation, proposal drafting from your past-quarter templates, and internal reporting. Claude Cowork lets your team work with an AI agent that has context about your specific MSP: your clients, your services, your ops. Not a ChatGPT prompt — a running system.
>
> **OpenClaw as the orchestration layer.** OpenClaw is where the automations live — n8n workflows that trigger when something happens (a ticket gets tagged, a client signs a new SOW, a proposal is sent). OpenClaw is the glue between Claude Cowork and your MSP's day-to-day systems. For MSPs that need more custom automations than a SaaS tool can handle, OpenClaw + Claude Cowork is what we install.
>
> **The unifying layer.** Everything runs on n8n. The outbound signal sweep that pulls Monday's account list is the same n8n instance that runs your client onboarding automation. One stack, two deliverables, shared infrastructure.
>
> **What you get at 90 days (target):** outbound engine live and producing qualified conversations, three to five internal automations running in production, weekly reporting you can read in 5 minutes, and a stack you can audit line by line.

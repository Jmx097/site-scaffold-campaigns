# Brief — plinkoenergy.com

The partnership motion. Plinko sources the conversation, VersaFile delivers the implementation. Mid-market Canadian utilities.

**Sensitive dependency:** This page requires VersaFile leadership sign-off before going live. Co-branding is not automatic.

## One-line positioning

Document management conversations for mid-market Canadian utilities — sourced by Plinko, delivered by VersaFile. One call, two specialists.

---

## Who this page is for (ICP)

**Job titles (primary):**
- Records / Information Management manager
- Operations manager (smaller utilities often have ops-own-records)
- IT manager or IT director
- Compliance lead (where the function exists at this scale)
- Owner / GM / Managing Director (at small IPPs and First Nations utilities, the owner is in the decision)

**Company profile:**
- Canadian electric utilities, IPPs, energy co-ops, First Nations utilities, district energy operators, small midstream
- **100 – 1,500 employees** (the gap between enterprise and small)
- Regulated under NERC, OEB, CER, or provincial equivalents — same regulatory complexity as enterprise, without the enterprise budget
- Running SharePoint + shared drives + Excel + manual file naming — and they know it's not going to survive the next audit
- Priced out of big-four consulting. Too small for enterprise ECM vendor attention.

**Buying trigger:**
- Regulatory audit coming
- Someone left, and their files are scattered
- An incident where a document couldn't be produced
- Owner-level frustration with the state of engineering drawings or contract docs
- Provincial regulator issued new requirements they can't meet on SharePoint

**Emotional state when they land:**
- More resigned than enterprise. They've accepted that big consultants won't call them back.
- Pragmatic. They want to know what it costs and what they get. Skip the capabilities deck.
- Skeptical of American vendors who don't know Canadian regulators.
- Slightly suspicious of "partnership" claims — they've seen channel-partner theater.

---

## Who this page is NOT for (anti-ICP)

- Non-Canadian operators
- Companies over 1,500 employees (route to versafilecanada.com)
- Companies under 100 employees (too small for M-Files deployment economics)
- Non-energy sectors (we don't know their regulators)
- Anyone who wants "just the tool" without implementation (M-Files unboxed is not the deliverable)

---

## Page promise (what the buyer walks away believing)

1. These are Canadians who know their regulator.
2. Plinko sources the conversation — this isn't a cold sales pitch, it's a structured intro from a firm that specializes in finding the right utilities to help.
3. VersaFile delivers the M-Files work — real implementation, not a reseller handoff.
4. The combined offer removes the "six-vendor shortlist, three consultants" dance that mid-market utilities don't have budget or bandwidth for.
5. A 15-minute call is the lowest-friction way to find out if M-Files is right for their environment — worst case, one honest opinion.

---

## What success looks like (acceptance criteria)

1. **Mid-market voice test:** The page doesn't sound like enterprise consulting (too formal) or MSP-facing Plinko (too casual). It lands in the middle — pragmatic, Canadian, peer-to-peer.
2. **Brand hierarchy is clear:** Plinko leads (sources), VersaFile is explicitly credited (delivers). Both names in the hero. Both names in the footer.
3. **The partnership isn't "theater":** The page says exactly what each party does. No "we work closely with partners to deliver solutions" mush.
4. **Regulator names present** (at least once): OEB, CER, NERC CIP. These are structural proof elements for mid-market buyers who are regulator-aware.
5. **Explicit anti-ICP:** who this isn't for, named.
6. **Three Cal.com CTAs + form + LinkedIn footer.** One primary path: `cal.com/jonmc/plinko-energy-intro`.
7. **No claims that require customer names we can't publish.**
8. **Mobile-clean at 320px.**
9. **CASL compliance.** Both Plinko and VersaFile mailing addresses in the footer — or a single combined address if approved.

## Voice and tone (from voice-and-guardrails.md)

| Dimension | Rating | What this means |
|---|---|---|
| Formality | 4 | Warmer than VersaFile Direct, more formal than tryplinko. |
| Technical density | 4 | ECM + regulator fluency assumed. |
| Assertiveness | 3 | Direct about what each party does. Not assertive about outcomes (no ACV or ROI claims). |

**Voice test:** Peer-to-peer Canadian. Not American-agency, not enterprise-consultant. An operations manager at a 300-person IPP should read it and think "OK, these people get it."

**Banned categorically on this page:**
- Gushing about the VersaFile partnership (credit once, reference once more in footer, done)
- "You deserve better" energy (too therapeutic)
- Agency-speak: "partner with you on your journey"

**Allowed but sparingly:**
- Light second-person ("If that's you" is fine)
- Peer shorthand ("you know the audit schedule")

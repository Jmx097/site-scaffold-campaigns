## Draft (new)

> **Is M-Files overkill for a utility our size?**
>
> Not if you're regulated. M-Files scales to single-business-unit deployments — records management alone, or engineering drawing control alone. We scope to what you actually need, not a full ECM rollout. Mid-market utilities at 100-1,500 employees are the operating scale VersaFile is built for.
>
> **Why are two companies on the call?**
>
> Plinko sources the conversation; VersaFile delivers the implementation. Two specialists, one call. If it's a fit, the engagement is with VersaFile and Plinko steps back. You're not signing one contract with two vendors — you're getting a structured intro without the six-vendor RFP process.
>
> **Who do we contract with — Plinko or VersaFile?**
>
> VersaFile. The implementation is a VersaFile engagement. Plinko's role ends at the introduction and qualification — we don't stay on the implementation scope or invoice.
>
> **How is this different from a big-four consulting engagement?**
>
> Priced for the mid-market. Scoped to what your utility actually needs, not a full enterprise transformation. And delivered by a Canadian partner who has shipped M-Files in this sector specifically — not a general ECM practice staffed by consultants rotating off other sectors.
>
> **What if we already use SharePoint — can M-Files work alongside it?**
>
> Yes. M-Files integrates with SharePoint and Microsoft 365 rather than replacing them. Your team keeps using SharePoint for collaboration; M-Files becomes the metadata-driven retrieval layer for regulated, engineering, and capital-project documents where retrieval against document attributes matters.
>
> **What does "implementation partner" actually mean here?**
>
> Configure the metadata schema and workflows to your regulator's evidence categories. Deploy M-Files in your environment, integrate with Microsoft 365 and any drawing systems. Train your records, engineering, and operations teams by role. Stay engaged for quarterly reviews, new document classes, and incident support post-go-live.

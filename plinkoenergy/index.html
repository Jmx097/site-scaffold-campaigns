The problem

Mid-market utilities sit on document systems that were built for a different regulatory load than today's. OEB filings. Engineering record retention. NERC audit readiness. Capital project lifecycle documents that outgrew SharePoint three capital budgets ago.

The options on the table usually look like:

  • Big-four consulting firms that price mid-market buyers out before the discovery call
  • Enterprise ECM vendors that sell a platform and vanish at go-live
  • Internal teams trying to hold it together with SharePoint and determination

None of those match the scale of the problem at a 100-500 person utility.

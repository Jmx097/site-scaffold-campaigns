# Process — plinkoenergy.com

## Intent

Describe what the first call + decision looks like, so the buyer can picture the next 30 days. If booked: call with the right people, structured recommendation, no pressure to sign anything. If not a fit: honest "no, here's who to look at."

---

## Draft (new)

> **What a first call looks like**
>
> **15 minutes, both sides on.** A structured agenda: your current document setup, what's coming up on the regulatory or capital project side, where the pain is highest right now. Plinko runs the call; VersaFile is there to answer implementation questions as they come up.
>
> **If it's a fit.** VersaFile scopes a discovery engagement — usually 2-3 weeks of workshops with your records, engineering, or IT teams to understand current state in detail, then a scoped implementation plan. Plinko steps back; you're now working directly with VersaFile.
>
> **If it's not a fit.** We'll tell you on the call. If M-Files isn't right for your environment, if the timing is wrong, or if your team's current setup is closer to working than it looks, we'll say so. Best case, you walk away with a second opinion on the document management question.

---

## Critique

- **Three clear blocks** — the call itself, what happens if yes, what happens if no. Matches sitemap intent.
- **"15 minutes, both sides on"** — sets precise expectation upfront. Good.
- **"Plinko runs the call; VersaFile is there to answer implementation questions"** — division of labor made concrete. Good.
- **"Plinko steps back; you're now working directly with VersaFile"** — honest about the handoff. Respects the buyer's need to understand who owns the relationship post-call. Keep.
- **"We'll tell you on the call"** — strongest line in the block. Matches the "honest anti-sell" guardrail. Keep.
- **"Second opinion on the document management question"** — exact phrase from cta-rules.md risk-reversal. Good consistency.
- **Voice check:** Peer-to-peer, direct, no hedging. Formality 4. Good.
- **"2-3 weeks of workshops"** — specific without over-committing. Matches versafilecanada process.md.
- **Consider:** The sitemap intent specifies "What happens after a qualified call: VersaFile scopes, Plinko steps back." That's covered. Also specifies "What happens if it's not a fit: honest 'no, here's who to look at.'" Covered but the "here's who to look at" is implicit. Could be more explicit: "If you'd be better served by an internal SharePoint consolidation first, we'll say so. If a global reseller would be more cost-effective for your scale, we'll say that too." Flag for rewrite decision.
- **No voice-and-guardrails violations.**

# Brief — tryplinko.com

The MSP low-commitment entry point. Founder-to-founder voice. The page that makes an MSP owner want a 15-minute call because the page itself is the proof.

---

## One-line positioning

A complete outbound engine for MSPs that can't justify a full BDR team. Book 2x more qualified conversations without hiring, training, or managing another human.

---

## Who this page is for (ICP)

**Job titles (primary):**
- Founder / Owner / Managing Partner of the MSP (this is who buys)
- Sometimes: Head of Sales, VP of Operations (where the MSP has grown beyond 25-30 people)

**Company profile:**
- 10 – 50 employee MSPs
- North American, English-speaking
- Services offered: managed IT, cybersecurity, cloud managed services, Microsoft 365 managed services, co-managed IT
- Annual revenue typically $1M – $10M
- Between "too small for a full BDR" and "growing too fast to do outbound between support tickets"

**Buying trigger:**
- Tried cold email from Gmail, burned the domain, stopped
- Hired a part-time BDR, didn't work out
- Tried buying a lead list from Upwork, got nothing usable
- Owner did 30 minutes of LinkedIn daily for two weeks, dropped it
- Referrals are flat and they need proactive pipeline

**Emotional state when they land:**
- Skeptical. MSP founders have been pitched by every agency on earth.
- Busy. They're scrolling this between Connectwise tickets.
- Allergic to marketing fluff. "Revolutionize your pipeline" → exit.
- Will respect honest anti-sell. "Not for MSPs under 10" builds trust, not loses leads.

---

## Who this page is NOT for (anti-ICP)

- MSPs under 10 employees (too small — we'd burn their domain faster than they can recover)
- MSPs over 100 employees (they need tryplinko's committed tier, route to dfyplinko)
- Non-MSP service businesses (accountants, SaaS, dev shops — different ICP, different language)
- Anyone looking for a lead list only (we don't sell CSVs; we run the engine)
- Anyone who wants to run it themselves (this is a managed service, not a tool rental — route to DIY options elsewhere)

---

## Page promise (what the buyer walks away believing)

1. These people have run outbound on their own MSP-adjacent business and know the signal-triggering game.
2. They don't hide the stack — tools are named, the routine is described, no "proprietary black-box" mystification.
3. The offer is explicit: 2x more qualified conversations, priced for a single-owner MSP to evaluate.
4. Anti-ICP is named honestly, which is itself a trust signal.
5. A 15-minute call is low-friction, no deck, no demo, no pressure.

---

## What success looks like (acceptance criteria)

1. **MSP founder test:** An MSP founder in a peer group (e.g., Evolve or Q4 peer group) forwards the link to another MSP founder without cringing at the copy.
2. **Stack is named openly** — Apollo, Hunter, HeyReach, Smartlead, n8n — with one sentence explaining why each is in the mix.
3. **The weekly routine is described** — not just "we do outbound," but what happens on Monday, what happens Wednesday, what the MSP sees.
4. **Anti-ICP is explicit** — "not for MSPs under 10" or similar, worded plainly.
5. **Voice is peer-to-peer** — sentence fragments OK, casual punctuation OK, no enterprise-RFP formality.
6. **Three Cal.com CTAs + form + LinkedIn.** Primary path: `cal.com/jonmc/plinko-msp-intro`.
7. **FAQ handles the top 5 MSP objections:** cost, commitment length, domain risk, what happens to replies, what happens if it doesn't work.
8. **Mobile-clean at 320px.** MSP founders read on phones between tickets.
9. **CASL compliance.**
10. **No AI-hype vocabulary** — this is the outbound-only page. "AI" isn't the hook here, outbound is. (dfyplinko carries the AI automation story.)

## Voice and tone (from voice-and-guardrails.md)

| Dimension | Rating | What this means |
|---|---|---|
| Formality | 2 | Peer Slack-message territory. Casual but crisp. |
| Technical density | 3 | MSP-literate. "Domain reputation," "BDR," "signal triggers" without glossary. |
| Assertiveness | 3 | Direct, confident, but not boastful. |

**Voice test:** If an MSP founder forwarded the page to another MSP founder without cringing, tone is right.

**Banned categorically on this page:**
- Enterprise-consultant vocabulary ("strategic alignment," "holistic approach")
- AI-hype ("AI-powered everything," "revolutionize")
- False urgency ("limited slots")
- Over-formality ("We thank you for your consideration")

**Encouraged:**
- Sentence fragments when they earn it ("Not Tuesday at 9 a.m. because 'that's when people check email.'")
- Self-aware punchlines ("You know why that gap exists.")
- Named tools in running prose — build trust by being specific

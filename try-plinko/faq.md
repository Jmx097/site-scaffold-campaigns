## Draft (new)

> **How much does it cost?**
>
> Monthly retainer plus pass-through for your sending infrastructure. Total monthly spend is comparable to a half-time BDR — without the hiring, managing, or ramp time. Exact numbers depend on target volume, geography, and whether LinkedIn runs alongside email. We share the full breakdown on the intro call.
>
> **What's the commitment?**
>
> 90-day initial window to prove the engine works on your ICP. Month-to-month after that. No year-long lock-in.
>
> **Will you burn my primary domain?**
>
> No. We set up warmed secondary domains through ScaledMail — separate domain, separate inboxes, 14-21 day warmup before any cold sends. Your primary domain never touches cold mail. If someone clicks unsubscribe in a cold email, it hits the secondary domain's reputation, not yours.
>
> **What happens to replies?**
>
> You see the qualified ones — prospects asking questions, asking for a demo, or giving objections we can't auto-handle. We reply to standard objections ourselves ("not now," "we already have a vendor," "take me off your list"). Unsubscribes are automated. Meetings book straight to your Cal.com.
>
> **What if it doesn't work?**
>
> We send you weekly reports with open rates, reply rates, meeting conversion, and inbox placement. If the trajectory is off by week 6, we re-target together — new signals, new segments, new cadence. If week 10 still isn't there, you can walk. Month-to-month after the 90-day window means no penalty for stopping.

## Critique

- **Headline is short, punchy, founder-friendly.** Good.
- **"A complete outbound engine for MSPs that can't justify a full BDR team"** — direct ICP call-out. MSP founders self-identify immediately. Keep.
- **"2x more qualified conversations"** — the central claim. Sourced in proof-library.md as "Our observation" with hedging required. Hedging currently missing in hero. Options:
  - Add "in our data" or "in our experience" to the claim
  - Or frame it less absolutely: "Book more qualified conversations — 2x is typical in our data — without hiring, training, or managing another human"
  - Decision required in rewrite pass
- **"Without hiring, training, or managing another human"** — punchy, concrete, peer-speak. Keep.
- **Button label** matches cta-rules.md ("Grab a 15-minute intro call"). Good.
- **Risk-reversal sentence** matches cta-rules.md. Good.
- **Voice check:** Formality 2, assertiveness 3. Right for this page. Contractions (it's, we'll) appropriate.
- **No enterprise vocabulary, no AI-hype.** Clean.
- **No named-tools moment in hero.** That's correct — the stack names come in solution.md as a proof moment. Don't front-load.
- **Brand:** "tryplinko" lowercase — consistent with domain. Consider whether to style it as a wordmark vs. plain text in HTML.

# Pain — tryplinko.com

## Intent

Name the specific gap that brought the MSP founder here — too small for a BDR, too busy to DIY outbound. The "tries-that-failed" list is the heart of the section: Upwork lists, Gmail sends, part-time BDR, daily LinkedIn dropped. Acknowledgment of what referrals give and don't give. Domain reputation reality.

## Draft (V1 adapted)

> **Who this is for**
>
> 10-50 person MSPs. Usually founder-led or recently past that phase. Selling managed services, co-managed IT, MSP automation, or cybersecurity.
>
> Stuck in the gap between "too small for a sales hire" and "growing too fast to do outbound yourself between support tickets."
>
> If you've tried any of these, you know why that gap exists:
>
> - Buying a lead list from Upwork and realizing half of it bounced
> - Running cold sequences from your primary domain and watching your Gmail deliverability tank
> - Hiring a part-time BDR who didn't stick
> - "I'll just do LinkedIn for 30 minutes a day" for two weeks before support tickets eat the routine
>
> Referrals keep the lights on. They don't create growth. Outbound is how you control your pipeline — but not if the outbound burns your domain or requires 10 hours a week you don't have.

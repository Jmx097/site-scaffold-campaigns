# Process — tryplinko.com

## Intent

Describe the weekly routine concretely so the MSP founder can picture what they'll see Monday vs. Friday. Founder's actual involvement: 15-30 minutes a week.

---

## Draft (new)

> **The weekly routine**
>
> **Monday, before you're awake.** An automated signal sweep pulls new accounts matching your ICP — companies that hit a trigger this week (a new ops hire, a PSA migration, a funding event). Enriched, scored, and queued for outreach.
>
> **Tuesday through Thursday.** Sends go out. Email from warmed Smartlead inboxes. LinkedIn touches through HeyReach on rotated profiles. Cadence timed for reply rates, not convenience — which means no 9 a.m. Monday spray to the world.
>
> **Friday morning.** You get a report: who replied, what they said, who's qualified, and what needs your 15-minute human answer. Meetings booked to your Cal.com calendar. Replies needing a judgment call in a single-click inbox.
>
> **Your actual involvement:** 15-30 minutes a week. Answering qualified replies. Showing up for the meetings we book. That's it. We run everything upstream — list building, enrichment, sending, reply triage, deliverability monitoring, inbox warmup rotation.

- **"Replies needing a judgment call in a single-click inbox"** — specific UI promise. Needs to be an actual deliverable. Confirm the reply-triage tooling exists at the right level by launch.
- **"15-30 minutes a week"** — commitment on founder's time. Needs to match reality; if actual involvement runs higher, hedge or remove.
- **"Answering qualified replies. Showing up for the meetings we book. That's it."** — strong, short sentences. Peer tone.
- **"List building, enrichment, sending, reply triage, deliverability monitoring, inbox warmup rotation"** — six-item ops list of what Plinko owns. Concrete, not vague.

## Per-section intent

### 1. Hero

**Job:** Stop the MSP founder mid-scroll. Make the offer legible in one read: 2x more qualified conversations, without hiring.

**Buyer state on arrival:** Skim-mode. Busy. Has been burned by agencies. Skeptical.

**Buyer state after this section:** "OK, this one talks like an MSP founder, not an agency. Scroll further."

**Must include:**
- Headline that names the audience (MSPs) and the outcome (more qualified conversations) in plain language
- Subheadline that frames the offer: the engine, managed for you, without hiring a BDR
- Primary CTA + risk-reversal ("No pitch deck. If it's a waste of your time, we'll tell you in the first five minutes.")

**Must not include:**
- AI-hype framing ("AI-powered outbound") — that's dfyplinko's story
- Enterprise-consulting vocabulary
- Generic "drive pipeline" language

---

### 2. Pain

**Job:** Name the specific gap that brought the MSP founder here — too small to justify a BDR, too busy to DIY outbound between support tickets.

**Buyer state on arrival:** "Does this actually match where I'm stuck?"

**Buyer state after this section:** "Yes. They've been in my shoes."

**Must include:**
- The specific tries-that-failed list: Upwork lists, Gmail sends, part-time BDR, daily LinkedIn commitment dropped after two weeks
- Acknowledgment of what referrals give you (stable volume) and what they don't (growth)
- The domain-risk problem — sending cold from Gmail primary domain is domain reputation suicide

**Must not include:**
- Shaming the founder for prior attempts
- Generic "pipeline problems" framing
- Fear-mongering about missed revenue

---

### 3. Solution

**Job:** Describe the engine with the stack named openly — Apollo, Hunter, HeyReach, Smartlead, n8n — so the MSP founder sees specificity and trusts the operator.

**Buyer state on arrival:** "OK, what's actually in this thing?"

**Buyer state after this section:** "These people run a real operation. They name the tools because they know what they're doing."

**Must include:**
- Tool names in running prose (not just badges): Apollo for firmographics, Hunter for email finding/verification, HeyReach for LinkedIn, Smartlead for sending, n8n for orchestration
- One line on why each is in the mix (build trust by explaining, not hiding)
- The managed-service framing: they don't see tools, they see results

**Must not include:**
- OpenClaw or Claude Cowork framing (that's dfyplinko's turf — don't dilute)
- A capabilities grid that reads like a services page
- Enterprise terminology

---

### 4. Process

**Job:** Describe the weekly routine concretely so the MSP founder can picture what they'll see Monday vs. Friday.

**Buyer state on arrival:** "What's the cadence? What do I have to do?"

**Buyer state after this section:** "I see what happens during the week. I know what my involvement is (minimal). I know when to expect results."

**Must include:**
- What happens Monday (signal sweep, list build)
- What goes out Tuesday-Thursday (HeyReach + Smartlead, timed for reply rates)
- What the founder sees by Friday (reply summary, replies needing their answer, meetings booked to their Cal.com)
- The founder's actual involvement: 15-30 minutes a week answering qualified replies

**Must not include:**
- Gantt-chart decoration
- Enterprise methodology language
- Vague "we handle everything" claims

---

### 5. FAQ

**Job:** Handle the top 5 MSP objections plainly, in founder-to-founder voice.

**Questions to answer:**
1. How much does it cost? (Answer: "We scope it on the call; ballpark range is $X-Y/month depending on volume" — OR: "We share pricing on the call once we know your target. Typical range is in the MSP-agency band.")
2. What's the commitment length? (Answer: 90-day initial window, month-to-month after.)
3. Will you burn my primary domain? (Answer: No — we set up warmed secondary domains via ScaledMail. Primary domain never touches cold mail.)
4. What happens to replies? (Answer: You see qualified replies, we reply to objections and forward interested prospects. You never deal with "take me off your list" — we handle unsubscribes.)
5. What if it doesn't work? (Answer: We report weekly. If the trajectory isn't right by week 6, we re-target together. If week 10 still isn't right, you can walk.)

**Must not include:**
- "Results vary by business" hand-waves
- Overpromising ("guaranteed X meetings")
- Pitch for dfyplinko tier inside an FAQ answer — cross-link in footer only

---

### 6. CTA

**Job:** Close with a casual, low-friction call invitation.

**Must include:**
- Final CTA headline ("Grab 15 minutes")
- Risk-reversal sentence
- Cal.com button + form
- Option to email directly as a fallback

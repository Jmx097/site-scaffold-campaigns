# Solution — tryplinko.com

## Intent

Describe the engine with the stack named openly — Apollo, Hunter, HeyReach, Smartlead, n8n — so the MSP founder sees specificity and trusts the operator. Tools in running prose, not just badges. One line on why each is in the mix. Managed-service framing: they don't see tools, they see results.

---

## Draft (V1 adapted, expanded)

> **What we actually run**
>
> - **ICP and account targeting.** Your real profile — headcount band, geography, tech stack, buying triggers. Not a 50,000-contact CSV you bought once and never cleaned.
> - **Multi-channel outreach.** Email and LinkedIn, orchestrated together so prospects see one coherent touch pattern instead of two disconnected ones.
> - **Signal-based triggers.** We send when something real is happening at the target — a new ops hire, headcount growth, a PSA migration, a funding event. Not Tuesday at 9 a.m. because "that's when people check email." Signal-triggered sends see 3-5x the reply rate of unsegmented cold lists per 2026 benchmarks.
> - **Reply triage.** You get qualified, ready-to-respond conversations on your calendar. We handle everything upstream. You close. We source.
>
> **The stack — no black box**
>
> Apollo for firmographics and contact data. Hunter for verified emails. HeyReach for LinkedIn outreach on rotated profiles. Smartlead for email sending on warmed secondary domains (your primary inbox never touches cold mail). A custom automation layer we built in-house on n8n that orchestrates the weekly routine.
>
> You see the full pipeline at all times — source lists, sequences, reply rates, inbox placement. No mystery, no wrapped-up black box you can't audit. Most agencies hide the stack because it's their only moat. We don't.

## Critique

- **Four-item engine description block** — matches V1 and works. Keep.
- **"Signal-triggered sends see 3-5x the reply rate of unsegmented cold lists per 2026 benchmarks"** — exact hedged wording from proof-library.md. Good.
- **"Apollo for firmographics and contact data. Hunter for verified emails. HeyReach for LinkedIn outreach on rotated profiles. Smartlead for email sending on warmed secondary domains."** — five named tools in running prose with one-line purpose each. Matches brief.md "named stack, no black box" proof moment.
- **"Your primary inbox never touches cold mail"** — specific, concrete, answers the #1 MSP domain-risk concern. Keep.
- **"A custom automation layer we built in-house on n8n"** — references n8n as the stack component; OpenClaw/Claude Cowork NOT mentioned here (reserved for dfyplinko). Good brand hierarchy.
- **"Most agencies hide the stack because it's their only moat. We don't."** — pointed, slightly bold line. Peer-tone, not preachy. Good closing.
- **Voice check:** Formality 2, assertiveness 3. Sentence fragment ("We don't.") works. Direct, crisp.
- **No voice-and-guardrails violations.**
- **"Custom automation layer"** is less specific than "n8n workflows we run every week" — consider whether to be more concrete. Tradeoff: too much detail becomes a tools-deep-dive; too little feels like wrapping. Default: current wording is right for the audience level (MSP founder, not CTO).
- **No dfyplinko-tier language** (OpenClaw, Claude Cowork, AI automation). Good — stays in the tryplinko lane.
- **No reference to dfyplinko** — brief.md allows cross-link only in footer; good.

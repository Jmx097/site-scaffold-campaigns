# CTA — versafilecanada.com

## Intent

Close with a named, time-bounded next step and risk reversal. The enterprise buyer has scrolled the page. They're either ready to book, ready to fill the form, or scrolling once more before leaving. Give them the lowest-friction path without pressure language. No newsletter. No gated PDF. No "chat with an expert."

---

## Draft (V1 adapted, with cta-rules.md wording)

> **The next step**
>
> A short call with a Canadian M-Files specialist. We'll tell you whether M-Files is right for your environment — and if it isn't, we'll say so.
>
> **[Book a 15-minute intro call →]**
> *No pressure. No mandatory demo. If we're not the right fit, we'll tell you.*
>
> Or share some context by email and we'll reply within one business day:
>
> [Contact form: Name, Email, Company, Message]
>
> [After submit] Thanks — we'll reply within one business day. Faster path: grab a 15-min call directly.
>
> [On failure] Couldn't reach the form server. Email us directly at jon@versafilecanada.com or grab a call at cal.com/jonmc/versafile-intro.

## Critique

- **"The next step" headline** matches cta-rules.md exactly. Good.
- **Risk-reversal sentence** matches cta-rules.md exactly ("No pressure. No mandatory demo. If we're not the right fit, we'll tell you.") Good.
- **Button label** matches cta-rules.md ("Book a 15-minute intro call"). Good.
- **"A short call with a Canadian M-Files specialist"** — emphasizes the Canadian context and specialist framing. Good for the enterprise buyer who's worried about getting a generic sales rep.
- **"We'll tell you whether M-Files is right for your environment — and if it isn't, we'll say so."** — reinforces trust, matches the "honest anti-sell" guardrail. Good.
- **Form has the required four fields** (Name, Email, Company, Message) per cta-rules.md. Good.
- **Success and failure messages** match cta-rules.md conventions. Good.
- **Direct email fallback included** — reinforces the "no dead end" CTA rule. Good.
- **Consider:** For an enterprise buyer, is there an appetite for a "longer conversation upon request" option in the form? Default: no (one path, one CTA). But note for iteration.
- **No voice-and-guardrails violations.**
- **Three Cal.com CTAs on the page** (hero, mid-page, this final block). Matches cta-rules.md Rule of Three.

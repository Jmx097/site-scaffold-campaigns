# FAQ — versafilecanada.com

## Intent

## Draft (new)

> **Why M-Files over SharePoint or staying on file shares?**
>
> SharePoint is built for collaboration — sharing, commenting, co-authoring. It's not built for metadata-driven retrieval against a regulator's evidence categories. M-Files resolves each document once and serves it to every context that needs it — asset record, regulatory artifact, maintenance reference, safety document — through different views of the same source of truth. You don't duplicate. You don't have a "which is the real version" problem. M-Files also integrates with SharePoint and Microsoft 365, so this isn't a rip-and-replace.
>
> **Why VersaFile over a global M-Files reseller?**
>
> Two reasons. First, Canadian context — we know NERC CIP, OEB, CER, AER, and provincial equivalents as configured realities, not abstractions. Second, implementation depth — we configure, deploy, train, and stay. A reseller sells licenses and hands off. An implementation partner builds the system to your regulatory and operational reality.
>
> **What does "regulatory-audit-ready" actually mean in M-Files?**
>
> Configured metadata categories that map to the evidence the regulator asks for. Retention schedules enforced at the document-class level. Full audit trails on access, modification, and workflow state changes. When the regulator requests "all engineering change approvals related to asset X over the past 36 months," the answer is a saved view, not a three-week manual compilation.
>
> **How long does a typical deployment take?**
>
> Most engagements run 12-19 weeks from discovery to steady state, depending on the number of document classes in scope and the complexity of existing-system migration. Smaller scopes — e.g., audit-prep records digitization — run compressed. Full-enterprise rollouts across multiple business units can run longer; we stage those in phases so each phase delivers value on its own.
>
> **What if our team can't dedicate 2-3 people full-time during deployment?**
>
> That's the norm, not the exception. We scope time commitment per phase. Discovery needs 3-4 workshops total. Configuration pilot needs a pilot owner at roughly 6-8 hours a week. Broader rollout varies by department. Our engagements are built around the reality that your records, IT, and engineering teams have day jobs.
>
> **What's included in "stay" — the post-go-live support?**
>
> A named delivery lead remains the contact for quarterly reviews, new document classes as your business evolves, configuration adjustments when the regulator updates requirements, and incident support. Support tiers range from full-service retainer (useful for the first year) to lightweight quarterly check-ins (common for stable mature deployments). Your team owns the system day-to-day; we're the specialists you call.

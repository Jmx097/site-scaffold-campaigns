# Hero — versafilecanada.com

## Intent

The enterprise ECM buyer arrives from cold email or a targeted search. They have 8-12 seconds before deciding if this is real or marketing spam. Prove within one scroll that this is a serious Canadian M-Files partner who works at their regulatory scale. No fluff, no "transform your business" energy, no stock-photo handshake. A records manager at OPG on their phone between meetings should read this and say out loud "these people have actually done this."

## Critique

- **Headline doesn't name the regulator.** Sitemap intent says NERC CIP, OEB, or CER should appear in the hero or immediately below. Subheadline has "regulatory records" generically but doesn't name a regulator. Missed structural proof element.
- **"Paperwork to be the bottleneck" is tonally off.** Reads like a CIO trade-magazine tagline. The buyer is a records manager or compliance lead who thinks in "audit windows" and "evidence production," not "paperwork bottlenecks." Replace with language the buyer would use internally.
- **"Built on M-Files" is good** — names the product, structural proof. Keep.
- **No version of "Canadian" in the hero proper.** The H2 has "Canadian energy and utilities" but the sentence underneath doesn't reinforce it. Buyer shouldn't have to infer from the domain name.
- **Risk-reversal sentence is approved by cta-rules.md.** Keep.
- **No voice-and-guardrails violations** — no superlatives, no hedge words, no AI-hype. Clean.
- **Button label "Book a 15-minute intro call" matches cta-rules.md.** Keep.
- **Consider:** One more line of concrete proof between subheadline and CTA — e.g., a named regulator, a named sector — to satisfy "evidence before ask" expectation of enterprise buyers.

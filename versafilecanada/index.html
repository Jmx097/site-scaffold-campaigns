Why M-Files for energy

Folder hierarchies break the moment a document is needed in two business contexts at once. An engineering drawing is simultaneously an asset record, a regulatory artifact, a maintenance reference, and a safety document.

Metadata-driven ECM resolves each document once and serves it to every context that needs it. No duplicate filing, no conflicting versions, no "which copy is the real one." Regulators, field technicians, and executives see the same source of truth through different views.

Canadian electric utilities, crown corporations, independent power producers, district energy operators, and midstream energy companies — the organizations we work with share one thing: the audit never takes a year off.

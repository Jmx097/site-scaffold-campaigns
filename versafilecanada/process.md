# Process — versafilecanada.com

## Intent

Describe what a real engagement looks like so the buyer can price time, effort, and ownership internally. Phases with rough time bounds. Who does what: VersaFile vs. client team. One line about what "stay" means post-deployment. No methodology trademarks. No Gantt chart. No hand-waving ("every engagement is unique").

---

## Draft (new)

> **What a typical engagement looks like**
>
> **Discovery (2-3 weeks).** We spend this window with your records, engineering, and IT teams understanding current state — what's in SharePoint, what's on shares, what the regulator last asked for, where drawings of record live. Output: a scoped implementation plan with metadata schema, integration points, and a phased migration sequence. Your team's time commitment: 3-4 workshops, roughly 2 hours each.
>
> **Configuration and pilot (4-6 weeks).** We build out the configured environment — object classes, workflows, permissions, integrations. Pilot scope usually targets one business unit (e.g., transmission engineering, or records management alone) so your team can evaluate on real documents before a broader rollout. Your team's time commitment: a pilot owner for 6-8 hours a week, plus a records or IT contact as-needed.
>
> **Broader rollout (6-10 weeks).** Phased migration into M-Files by document class, integration with Microsoft 365 and any drawing systems, training delivered role-by-role. We stage the cutover so your team has a working fallback at each phase — no single "flip the switch" moment.
>
> **Steady state and beyond (ongoing).** A named delivery lead stays engaged for quarterly reviews, new document classes as your business evolves, configuration adjustments when the regulator updates requirements, and incident support if something breaks. Your team owns the system; we're the people you call when you need the expert perspective.

## Critique

- **Four phases with time bounds.** Good — matches sitemap intent.
- **Time commitment from the buyer's team is explicit in each phase.** That's the differentiator — most ECM vendors are vague here. Keep.
- **Regulator reference once ("when the regulator updates requirements")** — inside the "twice per section max" rule.
- **"We stage the cutover so your team has a working fallback at each phase — no single 'flip the switch' moment"** — strong, direct, specific. Keep.
- **"Your team owns the system; we're the people you call when you need the expert perspective"** — clean closing line for the Stay phase. Keep.
- **"2-3 weeks" / "4-6 weeks" / "6-10 weeks"** — total approximate engagement 12-19 weeks. Is that accurate to real engagements? Should check against actual VersaFile deployment data. Flag for Jon's confirmation.
- **No methodology trademarks.** Clean.
- **No marketing superlatives.** Clean.
- **Consider:** Should there be a "smaller engagement" path for buyers who only need help with one capability (e.g., records digitization for an audit window)? Currently the description assumes full ECM rollout. May be worth a one-liner: "Smaller engagements — e.g., audit-prep records digitization — run on a compressed timeline."
